Good to know

Questions, answered.

Is the consultation really free?

Always, and there is no obligation to book. Drop in or ask for ten minutes when you reserve.

What if I need to cancel?

It's free up to 24 hours before your visit. Later than that, we ask for half the service price.

Do you work with every hair type?

Yes, from very fine and straight to thick and coily. Tell us about your hair when you book and we'll pair you with the right stylist.

Which products do you use?

Olaplex, Kérastase and Davines — and we'll tell you honestly what you need at home, and what you don't.

How do I get there?

We're a four-minute walk from BTS Nana (exit 3), with two parking spaces in front of the studio.
