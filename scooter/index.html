Ride safe, ride legal

The honest checklist.

  • LicenceThailand requires a motorcycle licence — your home one, or an International Driving Permit with the “A” category.
  • Helmet, alwaysDriver and passenger, every ride. It's the law, and the fine is ฿500 — but that's not why.
  • Keep leftThailand drives on the left. U-turns are common, and so are dogs — ride like everyone might surprise you.
  • Photos at hand-overWe photograph the scooter together at delivery. No arguments about old scratches, ever.

FAQ

Good questions.

Something else? Ask us on WhatsApp

Why don't you keep passports?

Because it's your most important document and hotels, immigration and banks may ask for it. We take a refundable cash deposit instead (฿3,000–15,000 depending on the scooter) and a photo of your passport.

What's included in the price?

Delivery and collection in Pattaya and Jomtien, two helmets, rain ponchos, a full tank, a lock, compulsory third-party insurance and 24/7 roadside assistance. No hidden fees.

How do the weekly and monthly prices work?

From 7 days the weekly rate applies to every day, from 28 days the monthly rate. The price builder above always shows the cheapest rate for your dates.

What if I have an accident or the scooter is stolen?

Call us first — we come to you. With zero-excess insurance you pay nothing. Without it, repairs are charged at our workshop's price list, capped at the deposit for damage.

Can I cancel?

Yes, free of charge up to 12 hours before delivery. Nothing is paid in advance, so there's nothing to refund.

Can I take the scooter outside Pattaya?

Anywhere in Chonburi and Rayong — Sattahip, Bang Saray, Rayong beaches. Just tell us so our roadside team knows where you might be.
