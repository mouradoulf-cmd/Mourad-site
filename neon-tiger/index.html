Before you come

Good to know.

Is there an entry fee or dress code?

No cover charge, ever. Shorts and flip-flops are fine — it's Pattaya.

How old do I need to be?

20+, as Thai law requires. Bring an ID or a photo of your passport.

Do I really not pay for pool?

Really. Order a drink and the tables are yours. Only the Monday knockout has a ฿200 entry, and it all goes into the prize pot.

How can I pay?

Cash in baht, Visa and Mastercard, or any Thai banking app with the QR at the bar.

Can we book the whole bar?

Yes — for birthdays, stag parties or company nights, up to 120 guests, with a DJ and your own playlist. Message us on WhatsApp.
