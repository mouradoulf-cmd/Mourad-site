One of the few real reggae bars left on the strip

Most of Soi LK Metro is clubs and speakers turned up to eleven. Trench Town is the other thing — a live band, a Caribbean-yellow doorway, and a crowd of regulars and first-timers who came for the music and stayed for the welcome. No stage script: call out a song, and more often than not, they'll play it.
