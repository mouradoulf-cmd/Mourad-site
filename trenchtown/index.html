One of the few real reggae bars left on the strip

Most of Walking Street is clubs and speakers turned up to eleven. One Love is the other thing — a live singer on the small stage most nights, a night-sky ceiling and hand-painted murals overhead, and a crowd of regulars and first-timers who came for the music and stayed for the welcome. No stage script: call out a song, and more often than not, they'll play it.
