02 The ventilated façade

Five layers,
one point of contact.

From insulation to panel fixing, including the substructure and anchoring: we supply, machine and advise on every layer.

  • 01Backing wall

    Concrete, masonry or timber frame: the backing wall that receives the façade.

  • 02Insulation

    Rockwool or Ursa rock wool, for optimal thermal performance.

  • 03Brackets and substructure

    InnoCLAD (aluminium), InnoCLAD+ (slab-to-slab), TradiCLAD (steel / timber), TradiCLAD+ (galvanised steel) or bespoke. Structural calculation notes and pull-out tests.

  • 04Ventilated cavity

    The space that makes the ventilated façade: air circulates behind the panels and moisture is carried away.

  • 05Cladding panels

    Fibre-cement, compact HPL, aluminium composite, compressed rock wool, timber: with visible or concealed fixing, machined to measure in our workshop.
