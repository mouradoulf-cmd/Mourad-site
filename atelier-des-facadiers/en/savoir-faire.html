Our expertise

Founded in 2015, Atelier des Façadiers grew out of an observation: cladding installers need to work faster and to a higher standard, at height and in conditions that are sometimes difficult.

01 Our story

From panel drilling
to complete solutions.

To meet these needs, we first offered panel cutting and drilling services. We then broadened our offer with stocks of accessories and complete technical solutions.

Today, we work with a wide range of materials, from fibre-cement and compact HPL panels to composite panels, as flat panels, cassettes or horizontal bands. We also supply substructure solutions for the most complex projects.

02 Our mission

Stylish façades,
durable and ready to install.

Transforming urban spaces into stylish, durable, energy-efficient environments, while making cladding installers’ work easier. Our conviction: the answer is ready-to-install. It improves façade quality, reduces the physical strain of the work and speeds up installation.

We work on housing, offices, shopping centres, commercial and public buildings (schools, swimming pools, sports halls) and complex projects.

03 Our pillars of excellence

What changes
on site.

Personalised support

Respect for architectural choices and technical studies: pull-out tests, sizing, structural calculation notes, and optimisation of panels (machining, engraving, perforation) and substructures.

Controlled costs

We use materials to the full to limit losses. Thanks to workshop preparation, we significantly reduce the time spent on site.

Efficient logistics

A large stock, available right next to your sites, and deliveries by self-unloading lorries to simplify logistics on site.

A responsible approach

Less waste thanks to workshop optimisation, and more safety and comfort for installers: fewer cuts and holes on site, less noise and dust.
