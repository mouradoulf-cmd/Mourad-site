Cladding
for façades

Fibre-cement, compact HPL, aluminium composite, compressed rock-wool, timber and composite timber panels: the full range, in stock and machined to measure.

Fibre-cement

Fibre-cement panels and boards for mineral, matt, durable façades.

  • Flat panels, cassettes, bands
  • Visible or concealed fixing (Tergo Design for Equitone)
  • New EQUITONE [inspira] range

Aluminium composite

Aluminium composite panels, light and precise, for flat panels, cassettes and bands.

  • Cassettes CH (Stacbond) and M-BASE (Alpolic)
  • Visible or concealed fixing
  • Folding, drilling, milling, lettering

HPL laminate

Compact HPL for highly durable façades, in a wide choice of finishes.

  • Eclip’s for Fundermax, TS200 for Trespa
  • Visible or concealed fixing
  • Made-to-measure cutting, drilling and grooving

Compressed rock wool

Compressed rock-wool panels, for understated, colourful ventilated façades.

  • Visible or concealed fixings
  • Workshop machining
  • Wide choice of colours
Timber

Timber cladding

Timber strips for warm, natural façades.

  • Strip installation
  • Installation accessories
  • Suitable substructure

Composite timber

Composite timber cladding boards, durable and easy to maintain.

  • Strip installation
  • Installation accessories
  • Suitable substructure

Full range (formats, colours, references) in the 2026 catalogue. Partner brands: Fundermax, Trespa, Equitone, Rockpanel, Stacbond, Alpolic, Fiberdeck, Neolife, Cedral, Haussermann, Gaiffe.
