About V6

A quiet room, made for slowing down.

Reception — photo placeholder
Treatment room — photo placeholder

V6 Thai Massage And Spa sits on Soi Lengkee in central Pattaya, a short walk from Nong Prue. The space is built around one idea: a proper Thai massage, done with care, without the rush.

Guests come back for the foot reflexology and the dermatological treatments as much as the massage itself — reflected in a 4.7-star average across 277 Google reviews.
