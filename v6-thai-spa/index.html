Good to know

Before you come.

What should I wear?

Nothing special. For Thai massage we give you loose cotton clothes; for oil treatments, disposable underwear and a towel. You're always covered.

Can I choose a female or male therapist?

Of course — just add it to your booking note and we'll arrange it.

Is Thai massage painful?

It can be firm, but never more than you want. Your therapist checks the pressure at the start and you can say “bao bao” (gently) at any time.

I'm pregnant — can I book?

From the second trimester, yes: we offer a gentle side-lying massage with therapists trained for pregnancy. Please tell us when you book.

Do you accept walk-ins?

Always, when a room is free. Evenings and weekends fill up, so booking a few hours ahead is safer.
