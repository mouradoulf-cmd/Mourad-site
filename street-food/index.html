    Our story

    One corner. One wok. Since 1998.

    Mae Lek — "little mum" — came to Pattaya from Isaan with her mother's recipes and a second-hand wok. She rolled her cart onto this corner of Soi Buakhao one evening in 1998 and never left.

    Today her son runs the grill, her niece pounds the som tam, and Mae still cooks every plate of kra pao herself. Twelve tables under the blue tarp, no air-con — and a queue of taxi drivers, which is how you know.

    Questions

    Good to know.

    Can I book a table?

    No need — it's a street kitchen, tables turn over fast. For groups of eight or more, send us a WhatsApp and we'll push tables together.

    Is the food safe for tourists?

    Everything is cooked to order over a very hot flame, ice comes from a sealed factory bag, and the stall has its municipal food licence on the wall.

    Do you have vegetarian food?

    Yes. Most wok dishes can be made with tofu and without fish or oyster sauce — say “gin jay” or use the card above.

    How does ordering ahead work?

    Add dishes with +, choose a pick-up time and send the order on WhatsApp. We reply with a thumbs-up and start cooking so it's hot when you arrive. Pay when you collect.

    Can I pay by card?

    Cash or Thai QR (PromptPay) only — there's an ATM at the 7-Eleven thirty metres away.
