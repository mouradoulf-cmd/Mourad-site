Pack 1 · Master AI

A chat AI answers your questions and writes text. Sign up free (limited daily quota) to at least two tools and compare.

  • Ask both the same question
  • Compare answers and pick your favorite
  • Careful: AI can be wrong, always check important facts

A good prompt = role + task + context + answer format.

You are a marketing consultant for restaurants (role). Write a Mother's Day promotion (task). Our restaurant is family-run with a small budget (context). Answer with a table of 3 ideas + one Facebook post (format).
Exercise: write a 4-part prompt for a real task, then improve it 3 times.

AI is great at: summarizing meetings, drafting emails, translating, answering customers, making checklists.

I will paste meeting notes. Summarize: 1) decisions 2) tasks + owner + due date 3) open issues. [notes]
Exercise: pick a weekly task and save the prompt for reuse.

Use an image generator with a free tier. Describe: subject, setting, style, lighting, angle, format.

a cozy street food stall at night, warm lights, shallow depth of field, photorealistic, 9:16
  • Do not use a real person's image or name without consent
  • Check each tool's commercial-use terms
  • Do not paste personal data or company secrets
  • Verify facts, numbers and legal rules at the source
  • Disclose AI use when a platform or client requires it
End of pack 1: ready for pack 2 (websites) or pack 3 (video).

Pack 2 · Make money with AI (building websites)

Start with local businesses without a site or with an old one: restaurants, salons, clinics, small hotels, tradespeople. Find them on Google Maps and Facebook.

  • List 30 businesses in your area
  • Note what each lacks (site, online menu, booking button, map)

Ask AI for a one-page site (HTML/CSS), then publish it free (GitHub Pages, Netlify, Cloudflare Pages).

Build a one-page website for [name], a [type of business]. Sections: header, about, menu/services + prices, reviews, hours/address/map, call/WhatsApp button. HTML+CSS+JS in a single file, [colors] palette, mobile-friendly.
Exercise: build a demo site for a business you know (your portfolio).
  • Real photos of the business (with permission)
  • WhatsApp/call button on the first screen
  • Test on a real phone
  • Business name + city in headings (local SEO)

Send the demo, not a long message.

Hi, I saw [business] has great reviews but no website. I prepared a free demo: [link]. Would you like me to adapt it for you?
  • 10 contacts a day, log results in a sheet
  • One follow-up after 3 days, then stop
  • One-page site for a small business: set your price by scope and local market
  • 50% deposit before starting, balance on delivery
  • Offer monthly maintenance (text/promo updates)
  • State the number of revision rounds in the quote
Real prices depend on you and the client: no rate is guaranteed.

Pack 3 · AI videos for TikTok

Steps: idea → script → visuals/video → voice → editing → posting. Check TikTok's help center for current monetization requirements (they change).

  • Label AI-generated content as the platform requires
  • No deceptive content, impersonation of real people or protected material

Many tools give free daily/monthly credits but cap the number, length, or add a watermark. Method: rotate several tools, assemble 3–8 s clips, edit in CapCut. Check commercial-use terms before posting.

Exercise: open 2–3 tool accounts, generate a 5 s clip, compare quality and quotas.

The first 3 seconds decide everything.

Write a 30-second vertical video script about [topic] in 5 scenes of 5–6 s: visuals / voice-over / on-screen text. Scene 1 is a hook that makes people want to keep watching.
  • AI voice with a free tier, or record your own
  • CapCut: auto captions, trim silences, licensed music
  • Export 1080×1920 (9:16)
  • Starting goal: 1 video a day for 30 days
  • Watch completion rate and where people drop off
  • Repeat what works, drop the rest
  • Income avenues: your own products/courses, affiliate, videos for businesses (results not guaranteed)
