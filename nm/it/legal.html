The care plan is optional. It covers hosting, your domain, unlimited edits on WhatsApp and keeping your content up to date. It renews automatically at the end of each monthly or yearly period until you cancel. Privacy policy

We collect as little as possible, and only to deliver what you ordered. This policy follows Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA) and, for visitors from the European Union, the GDPR.

What we collect

  • When you order or book a meeting: your name, business name and type, WhatsApp number, email, preferred languages and any note you add.
  • When you pay by card: Stripe collects your payment details on its own secure page. We receive the payment status, amount, email and the last digits of the card — never the full card number.
  • When you pay by PromptPay or transfer: the transfer slip you choose to send us on WhatsApp.

Why we use it

To deliver your order, contact you about it, send receipts and invoices, manage your care plan, and meet our accounting obligations. We do not sell your data and we do not use advertising trackers.

Who else processes it

Stripe (payments and invoices), WhatsApp (our conversations with you) and GitHub Pages (website hosting). Each acts under its own privacy policy.

How long we keep it

For as long as you are a client, then as long as accounting law requires for invoices. You can ask us to delete anything else at any time.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, export or delete your personal data, or object to how we use it, by sending us a message on WhatsApp. You can also complain to Thailand's Personal Data Protection Committee or, in the EU, to your local data protection authority.

Cookies

This website sets no advertising and no analytics cookies. Technical cookies can still be set when you pay or when you contact us on WhatsApp, as described below.

  • Your language is remembered in your browser's local storage so the site opens in the language you chose.
  • Checkout progress (the offer you picked and the details you typed) is kept in session storage until you close the tab, so nothing is lost if you go back and forth. It never leaves your device unless you send it to us.
  • Stripe sets its own cookies on its payment page, needed for security and fraud prevention.
  • WhatsApp opens on WhatsApp's own site (wa.me), which applies its own cookie policy and may set technical cookies there.
  • Fonts for Thai and Arabic are loaded from Google Fonts only when you switch to those languages.

You can clear this data anytime from your browser settings.
