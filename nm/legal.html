Terms of sale

1. What you buy

NM Studio sells four one-time offers — Google Business Profile, QR Menu, Complete Pack and Ultimate + Social — as described on the pricing page at the time of your order, plus an optional care plan billed monthly or yearly.

2. Prices and payment

Prices are shown in Thai baht (THB). Amounts in other currencies are approximate and for information only. Payment is made online by card, Apple Pay or Google Pay through Stripe, by Thai QR PromptPay, by bank transfer, or in person when agreed. Work starts once payment is received.

3. Delivery

Delivery times shown for each offer are typical and start when we have received your photos, texts and access we need (for example to your Google account). You review a private preview before anything goes live. Google decides on the verification of business listings; we set everything up and guide you through it, but we cannot guarantee Google's decision or timing.

4. Your content

You confirm that you own or have the right to use the photos, logos, texts and menus you send us. The professional photos we take for your offer are yours to use for your business.

5. Care plan

The care plan is optional. It covers hosting, your domain, unlimited edits on WhatsApp and keeping your content up to date (and, on Ultimate, social media management and the monthly video). It renews automatically at the end of each monthly or yearly period until you cancel. You can cancel anytime from your account or by sending us a message; cancellation takes effect at the end of the period already paid, and no further payment is taken.

6. Cancellations and refunds

If you change your mind before we have started work, tell us and we will refund your payment in full. Once work has started, the one-time offer is not refundable, but we will keep working with you until you are happy with the result within the scope of the offer. Payment disputes are handled through Stripe's standard process.

7. Liability

We build your listing, menu and website with care, but we cannot guarantee a specific number of customers, search rankings or reviews. Our total liability for any order is limited to the amount you paid for it.

8. Law

These terms are governed by the laws of the Kingdom of Thailand. If something goes wrong, contact us first — we will always try to find a fair solution quickly.

Privacy policy

We collect as little as possible, and only to deliver what you ordered. This policy follows Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA) and, for visitors from the European Union, the GDPR.

What we collect

  • When you order or book a meeting: your name, business name and type, WhatsApp number, email, preferred languages and any note you add.
  • When you pay by card: Stripe collects your payment details on its own secure page. We receive the payment status, amount, email and the last digits of the card — never the full card number.
  • When you pay by PromptPay or transfer: the transfer slip you choose to send us on WhatsApp.

Why we use it

To deliver your order, contact you about it, send receipts and invoices, manage your care plan, and meet our accounting obligations. We do not sell your data and we do not use advertising trackers.

Who else processes it

Stripe (payments and invoices), WhatsApp (our conversations with you) and GitHub Pages (website hosting). Each acts under its own privacy policy.

How long we keep it

For as long as you are a client, then as long as accounting law requires for invoices. You can ask us to delete anything else at any time.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, export or delete your personal data, or object to how we use it, by sending us a message on WhatsApp. You can also complain to Thailand's Personal Data Protection Committee or, in the EU, to your local data protection authority.

Cookies

This website does not use advertising or analytics cookies, so there is nothing to accept.

  • Your language is remembered in your browser's local storage so the site opens in the language you chose.
  • Checkout progress (the offer you picked and the details you typed) is kept in session storage until you close the tab, so nothing is lost if you go back and forth. It never leaves your device unless you send it to us.
  • Stripe sets its own cookies on its payment page, needed for security and fraud prevention.
  • Fonts for Thai and Arabic are loaded from Google Fonts only when you switch to those languages.

You can clear this data anytime from your browser settings.
