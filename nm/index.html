Questions

Everything you want to know.

Still unsure about something? Ask us directly — we usually reply within minutes.

Ask on WhatsApp
I'm not good with technology — is that a problem?

Not at all. You send us your photos and we take care of everything else — design, text, hosting and updates.

How long until my site is online?

A few days after we receive your photos and information — not weeks.

Can I change my photos or menu later?

Yes. Edits are unlimited and free — just send us a message whenever something changes.

How does the QR menu work?

Your customers scan the code with their phone camera — no app needed — and instantly see your latest menu.

I already have Instagram. Why do I need a website?

Instagram depends on an algorithm you don't control. A website is your own storefront: it shows up on Google, it's open 24/7, and it belongs to you.

Is the website really mine?

Yes — your name, your photos, your content. We build it for your business, not for us.

What if I want to stop?

There's no commitment. You can cancel anytime, free of charge.

Have you worked with businesses like mine?

Yes — restaurants, salons, spas and companies. Have a look at the live sites in the Work section above.
