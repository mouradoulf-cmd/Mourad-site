# Primo Bistro & Wine Bar (Jomtien) — prototype QR menu (UNOFFICIAL, made by NM Studio)

Prospect demo built overnight from the venue's **public** online menu. Nothing has been sent to the venue; the owner (Mourad) validates and sends the message himself (see `clients/primo-bistro/message.md`).

- 20 languages (names/sections translated by Claude — **to be proofread by a native speaker**), prices in baht, engine `menus/assets/`.
- Photos: Wikimedia Commons free licences (CC0 / PD / CC BY / CC BY-SA), picked and checked by eye, credited in `CREDITS.md`; labelled "illustration" in the footnote. Dishes without a faithful free photo are left photo-less on purpose (never another dish's photo).
- Footnote states: unofficial prototype, dishes/prices to be confirmed by the restaurant.
- Rebuild scripts: `menus/tools/prospect-demo/`.
